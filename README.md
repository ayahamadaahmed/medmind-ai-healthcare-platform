# MedMind 🩺🤖

### AI-Powered Multi-Tenant Engagement Platform for Healthcare Providers

MedMind is an AI-powered healthcare communication platform designed to help clinics, hospitals, and healthcare providers automate patient interactions through intelligent voice and chat agents.

The platform supports Arabic and English conversations and connects AI agents with appointment systems, provider data, healthcare knowledge bases, and communication channels.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

MedMind acts as an intelligent healthcare receptionist that can communicate with patients through:

- 📞 AI Voice Calls
- 💬 Web & Mobile Chat
- 📅 Appointment Booking
- 🔄 Rescheduling & Cancellation
- 🏥 Doctor & Specialty Search
- 📚 Healthcare Knowledge Base
- 📲 WhatsApp Confirmations & Reminders

---

> MedMind is designed to support healthcare communication and operations.  
> It does not provide autonomous medical diagnosis.
