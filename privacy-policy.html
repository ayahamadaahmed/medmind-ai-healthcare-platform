MedMind Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

MedMind is an educational healthcare technology project designed to provide AI-assisted communication, appointment management, and informational support for healthcare providers.

Information We Process

Depending on the service being used, MedMind may process information such as:

  • Name and contact information
  • WhatsApp phone number
  • Appointment information
  • Messages sent to the MedMind assistant
  • Healthcare-related information voluntarily provided by the user

How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide the requested MedMind functionality, including:

  • Answering user messages
  • Booking, rescheduling, confirming, or cancelling appointments
  • Providing healthcare provider information and FAQs
  • Sending appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Improving system reliability and performance

Artificial Intelligence

MedMind may use artificial intelligence to understand user requests and generate responses. The system is intended for communication and informational support and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Data Sharing

MedMind does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by authorized service providers that are required to operate the platform, such as messaging, cloud, database, and AI infrastructure providers.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, testing requirements, or applicable operational requirements.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their personal information by contacting the MedMind project team using the contact information below.
